about

I watch too many films, build things with machines, and take old computers apart to see what they were thinking.

Nineteen, Moscow, still a student on paper. Nights go to series I should have finished years ago, days go to neural nets that mostly work, and everything in between goes to hardware off the second-hand market.

The taste is fixed: dark fantasy without a happy ending, pixel art where you can count the pixels, black paper and one colour of ink. This page is that taste with a guestbook.
